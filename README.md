Problem Statement:

In today’s fast-paced digital environment, everyday readers often struggle to find engaging, bite-sized fiction that fits into their limited schedules and aligns with their immediate mood, as mainstream digital reading platforms predominantly cater to lengthy novels or generic content categories. At the same time, independent writers lack dedicated, community-driven spaces designed specifically to showcase short-form fiction and build a direct audience for quick reads. Without intuitive discovery tools-such as filters based on mood and estimated read time-readers find it difficult to maintain a consistent reading habit, while writers struggle with visibility. Storyvault aims to bridge this gap by providing an accessible, aesthetically engaging platform where readers can effortlessly discover short fiction tailored to their available time and emotional state, while giving writers a focused stage to publish and connect with their audience.
